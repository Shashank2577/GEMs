02 The problem

GeM is a large market, but bid teams spend most of their time before they even decide to bid

GeM handled more than ₹4 lakh crore of public orders in FY 2023-24, and thousands of bids go live on a typical working day. For a seller the hard part is not access. It is separating the few bids you can win from the many you cannot, and then getting each one right.

Where bids are lost
  1. Volume and noiseSomeone scans the portal every day. Relevant bids get found late, or not at all.
  2. Eligibility fine printTurnover, experience, OEM authorisation and Make in India clauses sit deep in long bid documents.
  3. CorrigendaChanged dates, specifications or terms are easy to miss once a bid is being prepared.
  4. Document chasingCertificates live in inboxes and on personal drives. An expired one is found on the closing day.
  5. Deadlines and reworkUndertakings are retyped and PDFs merged by hand at night. A slip can disqualify the bid.
GeM terms in plain words
EMDEarnest money deposit: a refundable amount that guarantees you will honour your bid.
ePBGPerformance bank guarantee: security the winning seller gives, usually a % of the order value.
MSEMicro and small enterprise (Udyam-registered). Often exempt from EMD and some turnover or experience rules.
Make in India Class-I / IILocal-content classes (≥50% / ≥20%). Class-I suppliers get purchase preference.
ATCAdditional terms and conditions a buyer adds to the standard GeM terms. Often where the traps are.
BOQBill of quantities: the itemised list of what the buyer wants, in what quantity.
CorrigendumAn official change to a published bid, such as a new date, specification or term.
OTP / DSC / e-SignThe ways a person legally authenticates a GeM submission: one-time password, digital signature certificate or Aadhaar e-Sign.

03 Our approach

A co-pilot, not an autopilot: TenderPilot prepares everything, and your team keeps the controls that protect your GeM account

GeM expects a named person to enter the price and authenticate every bid. Shared logins and bots that submit on your behalf risk your seller account. TenderPilot is built around that rule, so it does the most preparation for the least account risk.

09 Trust, security and compliance

Your data, your GeM account and your decisions stay yours: security is part of the design

Bid documents, financials and certificates are among the most sensitive files a company holds. TenderPilot treats them that way and keeps your GeM seller account out of reach.

A separate space for each company

Every company's bids, documents and analyses are kept apart. No other customer can see or reach them.

Role-based access

Owners, bid managers, analysts and viewers each see and do only what their role allows. You invite and remove people yourself.

Complete audit trail

Every analysis, override, document change, packet and submission record shows who did it and when.

Your data stays yours

You own everything you upload or create. Export it whenever you like, and have it deleted if you leave.

No GeM credentials stored

TenderPilot reads public bid information only. It never asks for, stores or uses your GeM login.

Enterprise-grade cloud

Hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure, with encrypted connections and continuous monitoring.
